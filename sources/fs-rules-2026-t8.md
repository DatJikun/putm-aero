## Cytaty kluczowe (skrót z raw)

**T 8.1.1** — *„A specifically designed structure mounted on the vehicle to guide the airflow around the vehicle, increasing the downforce acting on the vehicle and/or lowering its drag. The mounting of this structure is not regarded as an aerodynamic device, unless it is intentionally designed to be one.”*

**T 8.2.1 (FW / przed HR)** — *„…must be lower than 500 mm from the ground.”* · outboard przed osią: *„…must be lower than 250 mm from the ground.”* · za HR: *„…must be lower than 1.1 m from the ground.”*

**T 8.2.2** — poniżej 500 mm za osią przednią: *„…must not be wider than a vertical plane touching the most outboard point of the front and rear wheel/tire.”* · powyżej 500 mm: *„…must not extend outboard of the most inboard point of the rear wheel/tire.”*

**T 2.2.2** — *„Sliding skirts or other aerodynamic devices that by design, fabrication or as a consequence of moving, contact the track surface are prohibited.”*

**T 11.11.1** — *„The maximum combined total rated power of any active devices designed to move air is 500 W, this includes cooling fans but does not apply to CV 1.8.”*

## Implikacje tylko jako fakty z tekstu (bez decyzji Spec)

- Nasz RW (&gt;500 mm) siedzi w **wąskim** boxie (inboard tylnej opony), max **1,1 m**, max **250 mm** za oponami.
- Fan **nie jest zakazany** — jest limit **500 W**; „fan OUT” u nas to decyzja osiągów (Michalecki), nie ban z T 8.
- DRS: **cisza w T 8** + limity kontaktu z torem / envelope przy każdym setupie / sztywność / ewentualnie T 9 — nie „automatycznie OUT”.
